# Manual webcam acceptance checklist

These checks remain necessary. Record camera model, lighting, glasses, seating, settings, and observations. Do not infer clinical usability or typing accuracy from synthetic tests.

- Start with camera 0 and no visible face: show Looking for face, no movement or selections. Select a nonexistent camera in Settings: show Camera unavailable with a working fallback UI. Restore the device and Reconnect. Disconnect during capture and verify read-error handling.
- Perform six-step calibration. Check countdowns, multiple samples, rejection/retry with similar directions and missing blink samples, Cancel with old calibration, and retry with no old calibration. Move camera/seat and recalibrate.
- Verify physical look LEFT moves focus left, RIGHT right, UP up, DOWN down despite mirrored preview. A centered gaze should stop. Slight natural movement should remain in the dead zone. Hold direction: first repeat follows the initial delay, subsequent moves follow the interval. Higher sensitivity requires less movement.
- Default double blink: normal isolated single blinks should not select. Deliberate two complete blinks should select once and show confirmation. Try a blink below minimum duration, long eye closure, two blinks beyond gap, and gesture during cooldown. Verify closure does not move highlight. Record observed failures without claiming perfect separation.
- Begin a double blink, leave camera view, then return and blink once: no pending selection. Lose face while holding direction: stop navigation immediately; after recovery allow only fresh commands. Check stale-result behavior under a busy CPU.
- Gaze-select Pause. Only Resume and Calibrate should be available. Gaze-select Resume and verify text keys return; recalibrate hands-free from the paused board. No long-blink/head movement is required.
- Reach every letter, digit/punctuation, Case, ABC/123, Space, Backspace, New Line, Clear, Copy, Pause and Calibrate. Test row edges and vertical movement between uneven rows. Clear must offer gaze-reachable Cancel and Confirm. Test multiline text, cursor placement, selection replacement and natural scroll.
- Dwell: show progress; change focus, close eyes, pause or lose face before completion and verify reset. Hold a key after activation: exactly once. Leave and return to rearm. Pause must remain paused under steady gaze; look to Calibrate then back to Resume and dwell to resume. Confirmation layout defaults also require fresh navigation. Switch back to blink and verify dwell no longer selects.
- Turn sound off, adjust volume, enable navigation sound and selected-key speech. Check that held-direction repeats do not continuously sound, speech does not overlap/queue, unsupported keys and unavailable audio do not break typing. Verify no online TTS request is needed.
- Toggle preview: iris/lid landmarks and normalized debug measurements should be readable, only preview mirrored. No camera recordings are created.
- Resize to 900×800 and larger, with preview on/off and calibration visible. Check text area, large keys, labels and controls without overlap/clipping. Typing keys are hidden during calibration. Check high-DPI scaling and system font scaling separately.
- Mouse-click keys and type using physical keyboard. Confirm eye focus remains separate from text cursor and ordinary widget focus. Verify shortcuts do not interfere with ordinary arrows in the text area.
- Close while actively capturing, calibrating and awaiting a blink. Confirm window closes after worker completion, camera indicator extinguishes, and process exits. Repeat reconnect/shutdown. If the camera driver hangs, record that limitation.
- Restart: settings/profile restore, typed text does not. Try corrupted local JSON files and missing optional audio. Test a missing model in a separate copy: status directs setup, application remains responsive.
